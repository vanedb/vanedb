## Capacity scope decision — 2026-09-24

The maintainer approved splitting #210: completed capacity research and the
RFC 0008 amendment remain in 0.2.0. The complete unfinished physical-device
matrix is tracked in [#285](https://github.com/vanedb/vanedb/issues/285), in
0.4.0 alongside RFC 0008 (#203), after RFC 0005 quantization (#200) in 0.3.0.
It still requires resident memory, warm/cold p50/p99 and recall@10 for resident
f32, mapped f32 and binary navigation with f32 rescoring at 100k/1M and, where
hardware allows, 10M on a physical Android device and an NVMe laptop. The
20 ms p99 decision and IVF fallback are preserved. The allocator study does
not fulfill that matrix.

RFC 0003's current-engine competitor tables and RFC 0010's write/search
regression evidence remain required for 0.2.0. Closing the completed research
issue does not prove or waive those gates or the later device/mode experiment.
